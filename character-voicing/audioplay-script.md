### Session rules

- Record all dialogue dry, mono, 48 kHz/24-bit WAV; retain the original take.
- The Host and Archivist are fictional present-day voices. No performance is an
  imitation of a historical person.
- **AeonVoice handoff:** resolve `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR` and
  `AV-ARCHIVIST-EVIDENCE` in the separate `podcast-content` character registry.
  This script specifies only dramatic intent, pickup, and mix; the registry is
  the authority for voice-specific settings.
- This extract contains no historical quotation. Its statements about the
  object and its provenance must be checked against the board's source card
  before recording.
- The workshop and robot sounds are newly recorded Foley. They are not offered
  as recordings made with, or of, the historical object.

## Recording script

### A01 — Board 01: case-file opening — 0:00–0:12

**[SOUND ONLY]**

Black screen. A single dry contact click: `TATI-01`. Two beats of room tone.
`ARC-01`: a paper photograph is placed on a desk. `ARC-07`: quiet archive
room tone. The title is stamped on screen:

`LOGIC OF A PURE MYSTERY`  
`THE TATI ROBOT`

### A02 — Boards 01–02: the object — 0:12–0:40

**HOST — [NARRATION | `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR`]**

<!-- E15-H01-01 -->
This is an investigation into an object whose maker is not presently known.

<!-- E15-H01-02 -->
The photograph before us shows a small robot: a face, a chassis, mechanisms
arranged to be seen as well as to do work.

<!-- E15-H01-03 -->
It has come to be called the Tati robot. That name is a handle for this
inquiry. It is not an authorship claim.

<!-- E15-H01-04 -->
We will not turn resemblance into proof. We will not let a good story fill a
gap in the record.

### A03 — Boards 03–04: what is visible, what is not — 0:40–1:18

**HOST — [NARRATION | `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR`]**

<!-- E15-H02-01 -->
For now, the photograph gives us only what a photograph can give.

<!-- E15-H02-02 -->
There is a head. There are eyes. A jaw-like mechanism. A body with visible
structure. Those are observations, not a diagnosis of how the machine worked,
when it was made, or who made it.

**[SOUND ONLY: `ARC-02`]**

The photograph is lifted and turned over.

**HOST — [NARRATION | `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR`]**

<!-- E15-H02-03 -->
The available provenance places the object in Paris. It does not, by itself,
tell us the route by which it arrived there. Paris is a location in the file,
not a nationality or a maker's signature.

### A04 — Board 05: the empty field — 1:18–1:43

On-screen: `BUILDER: UNKNOWN`.

**ARCHIVIST — [NARRATION | `AV-ARCHIVIST-EVIDENCE`]**

<!-- E15-AR01-01 -->
Provenance is not authorship.

<!-- E15-AR01-02 -->
An object can be collected, sold, moved, exhibited, or remembered far from the
place where it was built. Until a source connects a person to this object, the
maker field remains empty.

**[SOUND ONLY: `TATI-02` contact click; `TATI-03` low motor strain;
`TATI-04` spring movement; `TATI-05` stalled motion; `TATI-06` silence tail.]**

### A05 — Board 06: evidence contract — 1:43–2:20

**HOST — [NARRATION | `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR`]**

<!-- E15-H03-01 -->
That emptiness is not a failure of the story. It is the condition of the
story.

<!-- E15-H03-02 -->
We can gather documents. We can compare documented machine cultures. We can
say when a connection is an inference rather than a fact. And we can build
transparent reconstructions to test a mechanical idea.

**ARCHIVIST — [NARRATION | `AV-ARCHIVIST-EVIDENCE`]**

<!-- E15-AR02-01 -->
But a reconstruction is not testimony.

**HOST — [NARRATION | `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR`]**

<!-- E15-H03-03 -->
Nor is a plausible builder a proven builder.

**ARCHIVIST — [NARRATION | `AV-ARCHIVIST-EVIDENCE`]**

<!-- E15-AR02-02 -->
The labels will stay on screen: documented, inference, reconstruction,
unknown.

**HOST — [NARRATION | `AV-HOST-INVESTIGATOR`]**

<!-- E15-H03-04 -->
Our question is simple: what kind of world might have made this machine
possible?

### A06 — Board 06: hold — 2:20–2:30

**[SOUND ONLY]**

Hold on `BUILDER: UNKNOWN` for ten seconds. No music. At eight seconds, allow
one quiet `ARC-07` room-tone tail to return. Cut to the first object-detail
board only after the silence has fully landed.
